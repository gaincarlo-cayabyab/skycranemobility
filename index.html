Privacy Policy & Philippine Legal Compliance

⚖ 1. Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)

SkyCrane Mobility Corporation strictly upholds the rights of individuals and corporate entities under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173) and the regulations issued by the National Privacy Commission (NPC):

  • Purpose of Data Collection: We collect contact names, business affiliations, mobile numbers, emails, and target flight GPS coordinates exclusively to conduct aeronautical feasibility checks, terrain obstacle assessments, and quote calculations.
  • Strict Confidentiality: All mission logistics data, terrain profiles, and corporate information are encrypted and never sold, leased, or disclosed to unauthorized third parties.
  • Rights of Data Subjects: Under R.A. 10173, you retain the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of your information by contacting our privacy compliance desk at info@skycranemobility.com.
✈ 2. Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP) Compliance

All commercial heavy-lift flights conform to Philippine Civil Aviation Regulations (PCAR) Part 11 governing Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems (RPAS):

  • Flights are operated exclusively by CAAP-certified commercial drone flight controllers.
  • Operations strictly respect authorized flight corridors, NOTAM advisories, altitude buffers, and ground exclusion safety zones.
  • All DJI FlyCart 100 aircraft undergo pre-flight telemetry verification and manufacturer airworthiness certifications.
🏢 3. DTI Business Registration & DOLE Labor Safety Standards
  • Department of Trade and Industry (DTI): SkyCrane Mobility Corporation operates as a duly registered business under Philippine commercial laws.
  • Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE): Tactical ground rigging teams and winch handlers strictly observe the Occupational Safety and Health Standards (OSHS) for zero-hazard site logistics.
