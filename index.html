Section 02

🧠 Simple Jargon Buster (What Do These Words Mean?)

A quick reference guide for everyday project conversations:

Complex Term Simple Explanation Why It Matters To You
Total Installed Project Cost (TIPC) The total money spent from start to finish on the entire job site, not just transport. Looking at TIPC shows your real savings.
Direct Logistics Premium The extra money paid directly for drone services vs. basic manual labor. SkyCrane costs ₱269,000 more upfront, but saves money elsewhere.
Site Preliminaries Fixed daily costs to keep a job site running (manager pay, generator fuel, guards, rentals). Faster completion saves ₱20,000/day in site fees.
Micro-Cracking Tiny, invisible cracks in solar cells caused by rough manual carrying up ladders/slopes. Causes panel failure later. Drones eliminate this risk completely.
Delivery Microcycle The time it takes to move one load from the ground to the installation zone. Drones take 15 minutes per load; manual labor takes 45 minutes.
Section 03

⏱️ Delivery Speed: Drone vs. Manual Labor

A step-by-step microcycle breakdown of how a single load of solar panels is delivered:

🚁 SkyCrane Drone Delivery

15 Mins Total
  1. Prepare & Hook Up (3 mins): Secure 2 panels (60 kg) in the protective carrier frame (10 kg total 70 kg).
  2. Flight Out (2 mins): Smooth automated flight at 10 m/s direct to the roof or hill.
  3. Unhook & Handover (2 mins): Quick-release unhook to the receiving crew.
  4. Return Flight (2 mins): Drone flies back empty to the landing pad.
  5. Battery Swap & Check (6 mins): Fast battery swap and safety check for the next run.
Result: 24 loads/day = 48 panels/day (Takes 5 full working days)

🏃 Manual Ground Delivery

45 Mins Total
  1. Loading (8 mins): Manual lifting and tying onto ground carts or manual hoists.
  2. Hauling (12 mins): Walking or dragging heavy panels across uneven ground or ladders.
  3. Unloading (5 mins): Manually carrying panels to the installation spot.
  4. Return & Rest (20 mins): Walking back and taking mandatory physical rest breaks.
Result: 12 loads/day = 24 panels/day (Takes 10 full working days)

Section 06

📲 Phone-Friendly On-Site Field Instructions

On-site checklist for field supervisors, ground rigging crews, and roof receiving teams:

1. Ground Staging Team Checklist

  • ✓ Maintain a clean, flat landing zone of at least 5 m × 5 m.
  • ✓ Keep non-essential personnel outside the safety perimeter cone (15 m radius).
  • ✓ Load max 2 modules (60 kg) into the rigid frame. Total weight must stay under 70 kg.
  • ✓ Inspect latch pins before giving the visual "Thumbs Up" signal to the pilot.

2. Roof / Receiving Team Checklist

  • ✓ Wear hard hats, high-vis vests, safety harnesses, and cut-resistant gloves.
  • ✓ Stand clear until the payload is stabilized at chest height (1 m off surface).
  • ✓ Unhook payload using quick-release carabiners.
  • ✓ Give clear hand signal to pilot once the hook clear distance is over 2 m.

3. Operational Limits (Safety Rules)

💨 Max Wind Speed: 12 m/s (approx. 43 km/h)
🌧️ Rain Policy: Immediate pause if heavy rainfall begins
⚡ Battery Rotation: Min 4 sets on active fast-chargers (9-min cycle)

Executive Summary & Core Concept

Traditional procurement focuses exclusively on Freight Cost Per Kilometer — creating a major oversight by ignoring hidden site-preparation and operational delay costs. SkyCrane uses the TIPC Paradigm to reveal the true total expenditure required.

Total Installed Project Cost Formula

TIPC = Direct Transport Fee + Civil Works & Site Access
+ Labor & Rigging + Delay Penalties + Environmental Restoration

💡 Key Insight: By flying above terrain obstacles, SkyCrane eliminates ground infrastructure costs entirely — delivering cargo directly to the drop zone in hours rather than weeks.
