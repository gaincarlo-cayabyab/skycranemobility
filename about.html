The SkyCrane Mission

Where Ground Infrastructure Ends, Our Flights Begin.

The Philippines is an archipelago of over 7,600 islands characterized by towering mountain ranges, volcanic slopes, coastal gorges, and dense rainforests. Traditional industrial hauling requires millions in road construction, bulldozing, and weeks of hazardous ground transport.

SkyCrane Mobility was established to change this equation forever. By pairing cutting-edge heavy-lift autonomous aerial platforms like the DJI FlyCart 100 with CAAP-certified RPAS flight commanders, we transport 85 kg structural payloads, energy gear, and life-saving relief across impassable gorges in minutes instead of days.

Privacy Policy & Philippine Legal Compliance

SkyCrane Mobility Corporation • R.A. 10173 (DPA) • CAAP PCAR Part 11 • DTI • DOLE OSHS Standards

1. Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)

SkyCrane Mobility Corporation strictly upholds the rights of individuals and corporate entities under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and all circulars issued by the National Privacy Commission (NPC):

  • Lawful Basis & Purpose of Collection: Client names, corporate titles, official emails, contact numbers, and flight target coordinates (GPS coordinates, elevation, and terrain maps) are processed exclusively for conducting aeronautical feasibility reviews, terrain hazard assessments, and official commercial quote calculations.
  • Information Security & Confidentiality: All flight profiles, logistics payloads, and commercial communications are encrypted in transit and at rest. SkyCrane Mobility never sells, leases, trades, or discloses sensitive client parameters to third parties without prior express written authorization.
  • Data Subject Rights (R.A. 10173, Chapter III): You retain the legal right to be informed, access your personal records, rectify errors, and request permanent erasure of your profile. Direct all privacy inquiries to our compliance desk: info@skycranemobility.com.
2. Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP) Compliance

All heavy-lift UAV flights conform to Philippine Civil Aviation Regulations (PCAR) Part 11 governing Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems (RPAS):

  • Certified Flight Controllers: Flights are commanded exclusively by CAAP-licensed commercial drone operators with Category 3 Heavy-Lift RPAS ratings.
  • Flight Corridors & NOTAMs: Operations strictly observe authorized flight corridors, NOTAM advisories, altitude buffers, and ground exclusion safety perimeters.
  • Airworthiness & Telemetry: All DJI FlyCart 100 aircraft undergo pre-flight telemetry verification, radar obstacle avoidance checks, and manufacturer airworthiness certifications.
3. DTI Registration & DOLE Labor Safety Standards
  • Department of Trade and Industry (DTI): SkyCrane Mobility Corporation operates as a duly registered business under Philippine commercial laws with full tax and corporate registry compliance.
  • Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE): Tactical ground rigging teams and winch operators strictly observe the Occupational Safety and Health Standards (OSHS) for zero-hazard site logistics.
