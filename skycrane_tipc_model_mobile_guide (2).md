## ⏱️ Delivery Speed: Drone vs. Manual Labor

Here is a side-by-side breakdown of how a single load of solar panels is delivered.

### 🚁 SkyCrane Drone Delivery (15 Mins Total)
1. **Prepare & Hook Up** (3 mins): Secure 2 panels ($60\text{ kg}$) in the protective carrier frame ($10\text{ kg}$).
2. **Flight Out** (2 mins): Smooth automated flight at $10\text{ m/s}$ direct to the roof/hill.
3. **Unhook & Handover** (2 mins): Quick-release unhook to the receiving crew.
4. **Return Flight** (2 mins): Drone flies back empty to the landing pad.
5. **Battery Swap & Check** (6 mins): Fast battery swap and safety check for the next run.

### 🏃 Manual Ground Delivery (45 Mins Total)
1. **Loading** (8 mins): Manual lifting and tying onto ground carts or hoists.
2. **Hauling** (12 mins): Walking or dragging heavy panels across uneven ground or ladders.
3. **Unloading** (5 mins): Manually carrying panels to the installation spot.
4. **Return & Rest** (20 mins): Walking back and taking mandatory physical rest breaks.

## 📲 Phone-Friendly On-Site Field Instructions

If you are on site, follow these simple visual steps to keep operations safe and fast:

### 1. Ground Staging Team Checklist
- [ ] Maintain a clean, flat landing zone of at least $5\text{ m} \times 5\text{ m}$.
- [ ] Keep non-essential personnel outside the safety perimeter cone ($15\text{ m}$ radius).
- [ ] Load max 2 modules ($60\text{ kg}$) into the rigid frame. Total weight must stay under $70\text{ kg}$.
- [ ] Inspect latch pins before giving the visual "Thumbs Up" signal to the pilot.

### 2. Roof / Receiving Team Checklist
- [ ] Wear hard hats, high-vis vests, safety harnesses, and cut-resistant gloves.
- [ ] Stand clear until the payload is stabilized at chest height ($1\text{ m}$ off surface).
- [ ] Unhook payload using quick-release carabiners.
- [ ] Give clear hand signal to pilot once the hook clear distance is over $2\text{ m}$.

### 3. Operational Limits (Safety Rules)
- 💨 **Max Wind Speed**: $12\text{ m/s}$ (approx. $43\text{ km/h}$)
- 🌧️ **Rain Policy**: Immediate pause if heavy rainfall begins.
- ⚡ **Battery Rotation**: Minimum 4 battery sets on active fast-chargers ($9\text{ min}$ charge cycle).
