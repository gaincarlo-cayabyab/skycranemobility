## 2. Footer Redundancy Audit & Benchmarking

### 2.1 Problem Analysis

In the previous draft, the footer section repeated content already displayed in the **Headquarters & Dispatch** section and the main sticky navigation bar:

* **Duplication Identified:**

  * Address text (`08 BDEE Bldg. Perez Blvd., Herrero, Dagupan City, Pangasinan`) was duplicated word-for-word from the Headquarters section.

  * Generic top-level navigation (`About Us • Solutions • Fleet • Compliance • Contact`) duplicated the primary menu bar without providing deep links or secondary utility.

### 2.2 Enterprise Industry Benchmarking

Analysis of premier UAV, aerospace, and logistics platforms (such as DJI Enterprise, Zipline, Skydio, and Joby Aviation) reveals that effective footers serve as **functional utility centers** rather than simple contact repeaters. Key components include:

1. **Categorized Ecosystem Navigation:** Deep links into specific services, regulatory compliance, and fleet specs.

2. **Regulatory & Flight Safety Credentials:** CAAP (Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines) operational compliance badges.

3. **Command & Emergency Contact:** Dedicated emergency flight dispatch / hotline separate from general inquiries.

4. **Newsletter / Insights Subscription:** Industry whitepapers, flight log updates, and regional expansion announcements.

5. **Legal & Security Disclosures:** Privacy Policy, Terms of Flight Operations, Security, and Cookie Preferences.
