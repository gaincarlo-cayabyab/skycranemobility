Regulatory Compliance Framework

Four Pillars of Operational Trust

SkyCrane Mobility Corporation (OPC) is built on an enterprise compliance framework that allows large EPC contractors, government agencies, and utility operators to fast-track internal safety and risk reviews.

✈

1. Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP)

PCAR Part 11 • Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems (RPAS)

Heavy-lift drone operations in the Philippines are strictly regulated under CAAP regulations. SkyCrane operates with certified pilots, approved NOTAM corridors, and aircraft airworthiness compliance:

  • ✓ Category 3 Heavy-Lift RPAS Ratings: Licensed flight crew qualified for operations exceeding 25 kg MTOW (DJI FlyCart 100 MTOW up to 133 kg).
  • ✓ Airspace Authorization & NOTAMs: Pre-coordinated Notice to Airmen (NOTAM) corridors with CAAP air traffic management for high-altitude mountain runs.
  • ✓ Digital Telemetry & Geo-fencing: Automated flight data logging with redundant GNSS fail-safes and Return-to-Home (RTH) protocols.
🛡️

2. DOLE Occupational Safety & Health (OSHS)

Department of Labor and Employment • Rigging & Work-at-Height

Replacing hazardous manual carrying on 45-degree rocky inclines eliminates one of the leading causes of workplace injuries in remote infrastructure:

  • ✓ Zero Height Exposure: Eliminates human portage across unstable scree, cliffs, and temporary mountain ladders.
  • ✓ Certified Tactical Rigging: High-tensile load straps, dynamic center-of-gravity ballast checks, and automated cable winch anti-sway controllers.
  • ✓ Ground Safety Exclusions: 30-meter radial drop-zone safety perimeter maintained during all 20m winch hoist deployments.
⚖

3. Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173)

National Privacy Commission (NPC) Compliance

Industrial infrastructure data—including topographical coordinates, high-voltage substation vectors, and solar array blueprints—is treated as strictly protected:

  • ✓ Confidential Coordinates: Client GPS waypoints and site elevation profiles are encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • ✓ No Unauthorized Media Sharing: On-board optical and LiDAR inspection data belongs strictly to the client under formal NDA.
  • ✓ NPC Compliant Record Keeping: Data subject rights under Chapter III of R.A. 10173 are fully preserved across all digital touchpoints.
💼

4. Comprehensive Aviation Insurance & Underwriting

Pioneer Insurance • Malayan Insurance Aviation Cover

Commercial enterprise flights are backed by institutional underwriting to guarantee complete financial and liability protection:

  • ✓ Third-Party Liability (TPL): Broad commercial aviation liability coverage for all ground perimeters and public infrastructure.
  • ✓ Hull & Equipment Policy: Complete replacement underwriting for our DJI FlyCart 100 fleet and precision winch systems.
  • ✓ Custom Cargo Riders: High-value cargo coverage available for specialized equipment such as solar inverters, fiber reels, and satellite terminals.

Privacy Policy & Philippine Legal Compliance

SkyCrane Mobility Corporation • R.A. 10173 (DPA) • CAAP PCAR Part 11 • DTI • DOLE OSHS Standards

1. Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)

SkyCrane Mobility Corporation strictly upholds the rights of individuals and corporate entities under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and all circulars issued by the National Privacy Commission (NPC):

  • Lawful Basis & Purpose of Collection: Client names, corporate titles, official emails, contact numbers, and flight target coordinates (GPS coordinates, elevation, and terrain maps) are processed exclusively for conducting aeronautical feasibility reviews, terrain hazard assessments, and official commercial quote calculations.
  • Information Security & Confidentiality: All flight profiles, logistics payloads, and commercial communications are encrypted in transit and at rest. SkyCrane Mobility never sells, leases, trades, or discloses sensitive client parameters to third parties without prior express written authorization.
  • Data Subject Rights (R.A. 10173, Chapter III): You retain the legal right to be informed, access your personal records, rectify errors, and request permanent erasure of your profile. Direct all privacy inquiries to our compliance desk: info@skycranemobility.com.
2. Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP) Compliance

All heavy-lift UAV flights conform to Philippine Civil Aviation Regulations (PCAR) Part 11 governing Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems (RPAS):

  • Certified Flight Controllers: Flights are commanded exclusively by CAAP-licensed commercial drone operators with Category 3 Heavy-Lift RPAS ratings.
  • Flight Corridors & NOTAMs: Operations strictly observe authorized flight corridors, NOTAM advisories, altitude buffers, and ground exclusion safety perimeters.
  • Airworthiness & Telemetry: All DJI FlyCart 100 aircraft undergo pre-flight telemetry verification, radar obstacle avoidance checks, and manufacturer airworthiness certifications.
3. DTI Registration & DOLE Labor Safety Standards
  • Department of Trade and Industry (DTI): SkyCrane Mobility Corporation operates as a duly registered business under Philippine commercial laws with full tax and corporate registry compliance.
  • Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE): Tactical ground rigging teams and winch operators strictly observe the Occupational Safety and Health Standards (OSHS) for zero-hazard site logistics.
