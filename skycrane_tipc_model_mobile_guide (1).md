## 2. How the Model Works (Step-by-Step for Users)

Here is how the TIPC Model calculates your true savings in 4 simple steps:

### Step 1: Transport Speed & Microcycle Time
* **Ground Transport (Manual + Hoist):** Take $45 \text{ minutes}$ per load due to walking across rough terrain, climbing ladders, and manual maneuvering.
  * *Result:* $12 \text{ loads/day} = 24 \text{ panels/day}$. Takes **10 full working days**.
* **SkyCrane Drone Logistics:** Takes only $15 \text{ minutes}$ per round-trip flight.
  * *Result:* $24 \text{ loads/day} = 48 \text{ panels/day}$. Takes only **5 full working days**.

### Step 2: Site Overhead & Daily Preliminaries Savings
Running a construction site costs money every single day (site managers, safety officers, generator power, security, container rentals).
* Average site overhead cost: **₱20,000 per day**.
* Ground method (10 days): $10 \times \text{₱}20,000 = \text{₱}200,000$.
* SkyCrane method (5 days): $5 \times \text{₱}20,000 = \text{₱}100,000$.
* **Savings on Site Overhead:** **₱100,000 saved**.

### Step 3: Eliminating Temporary Access Roads & Scaffolding
* Ground transport often requires building temporary dirt roads, laying heavy rubber mats, or erecting temporary scaffolding so workers can safely carry heavy panels up or across uneven ground.
* Average temporary civil works cost: **₱300,000**.
* **SkyCrane flies over all obstacles**, meaning **₱0 spent on temporary roads or scaffolding**.

### Step 4: Quality & Risk Protection (Hidden Bonus Savings)
* **Zero Micro-Cracking:** Carrying heavy panels up ladders often creates hidden microscopic cracks in silicon solar cells, shortening their 25-year lifespan. SkyCrane moves panels smoothly in a rigid cradle.
* **Zero High-Elevation Hazards:** Workers aren't carrying $30 \text{ kg}$ glass panels up dangerous heights or steep hills.
